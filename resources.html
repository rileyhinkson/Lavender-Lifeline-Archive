Find Care

Resources for the community

Providers, hotlines, and directories for trans and LGBTQ+ people seeking affirming healthcare. Links are verified active — if something is down, let us know.

Telehealth Providers — National
↗
🪷
Plume
Trans-led telehealth for gender-affirming hormone therapy. Informed consent model — no therapist letter required. Available in 47 states.
HRT Telehealth Trans-led
↗
🏳️‍🌈
FOLX Health
LGBTQ+-by-LGBTQ+ clinicians. HRT, PrEP, primary care, mental health, and sexual health — all via telehealth. Over 1 in 3 clinicians identify as trans or nonbinary.
HRT PrEP Primary Care Telehealth
↗
🩺
Planned Parenthood
Gender-affirming hormone therapy at most health centers nationwide, many offering telehealth. Informed consent, sliding scale fees, most insurance accepted.
HRT In-person + Telehealth Sliding Scale
In-Person & Regional Providers
↗
🏥
Central Outreach Wellness Center
Western PA & Ohio. Gender-affirming care, HIV/Hep C treatment, PrEP, mental health — no insurance required. 9 locations including Pittsburgh North Shore.
Pittsburgh + OH HRT HIV Care No Insurance OK
↗
🏳️‍⚧️
Howard Brown Health
Chicago-based. Comprehensive trans & gender diverse health including hormone therapy, surgery navigation, mental health, and HIV services.
Chicago, IL HRT Surgery Nav HIV Care
Legal Aid & Rights Organizations
↗
⚖️
Lambda Legal
National nonprofit legal organization pursuing litigation, education, and policy work on behalf of LGBTQ+ people and those living with HIV. Provides legal help referrals.
Legal Aid National Free
↗
📋
National Center for Transgender Equality
Policy advocacy organization with free know-your-rights guides, state-by-state ID document change instructions, and federal policy updates for transgender people.
Know Your Rights ID Documents Free
↗
🛡️
ACLU LGBTQ+ Rights
Legal representation, impact litigation, and know-your-rights resources for LGBTQ+ people. State affiliates provide local legal referrals in addition to national staff.
Legal Aid National Free
Financial Assistance
↗
🎗️
Point of Pride
Annual HRT Access Fund and free chest binder program for trans people who cannot afford them. Applications open on an annual cycle — see site for current dates.
HRT Assistance Binders Free
↗
💜
Trans Lifeline Microgrants
Peer-to-peer grants for trans people facing financial hardship. Funds legal name and gender marker changes and other urgent needs. Run by trans people.
Grants Name Change Trans-run
↗
🏥
Jim Collins Foundation
Grants for gender-affirming surgical procedures for transgender people who cannot afford them. Annual application cycle — check site for current grant availability.
Surgery Grants Annual Free
Directories, Databases & Crisis Support
↗
🔍
OutCare OutList
National directory of LGBTQ+-affirming healthcare providers. Search by location, specialty, and identity. Run by OutCare Health, a nonprofit focused on LGBTQ+ health equity.
Provider Directory National
↗
🪪
NCTE ID Documents Center
State-by-state instructions for updating name and gender marker on driver's license, passport, Social Security record, and birth certificate. Maintained by the National Center for Transgender Equality.
ID Documents Name Change National
↗
📞
Trans Lifeline
Peer support hotline run by trans people, for trans people. US: 877-565-8860  |  Canada: 877-330-6366. Not just for crises — call if you need someone trans to talk to.
Crisis Support Peer-run Trans-only Operators
↗
🤝
The Trevor Project
Crisis intervention and suicide prevention for LGBTQ+ youth under 25. TrevorLifeline: 1-866-488-7386. TrevorText: text START to 678-678. 24/7.
Crisis Support Youth (under 25) 24/7
