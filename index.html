Our story

Built in a bedroom rack,
maintained with community in mind

This is not a lab for gaming servers. This is a lab with the hope to protect life-saving research.

Built in a bedroom rack, maintained with community in mind — because the internet forgets, institutions fail, and some of us can't afford to lose this information.

The Lavender Lifeline Archive was started by a trans technologist who watched institutions deprioritize, defund, and quietly delete the research that keeps marginalized people alive and informed.

This is for anyone who has ever needed medical information and found the link broken, the study paywalled, or the organization defunded. The archive is being built in public. Follow along.
